About Hexacore

Precision Metrology, Born on the Shop Floor.

Hexacore was founded to bridge the gap between machine maintenance and metrological certainty. We spent years witnessing high-performance CNC equipment underperform due to thermal drift, structural distortion, and kinematic error, all invisible to standard maintenance checks.

Our methodology combines hands-on engineering with traceable measurement technologies. We operate as an extension of your engineering team, providing actionable diagnostic data that empowers you to move beyond reactive repairs toward proactive accuracy management.

Mission

To eliminate uncertainty in manufacturing by providing laser-calibrated benchmarks that define machine performance and reduce tolerance-related waste across the industry.

Vision

To be the definitive standard for machine accuracy services in South Asia, where 'Hexacore-calibrated' is a recognised marker of a machine operating at its theoretical design limit.

Facility Floor — Metrology & Precision Analysis

The Value We Bring

  • Traceability: Full report documentation mapped to national standards.
  • Process Efficiency: Drastically reduced rework through geometric optimisation.
  • Diagnostics: Advanced kinematics analysis for multi-axis systems.
  • Transparency: Unbiased technical reporting—if it doesn't need fixing, we don't fix it.

Why Choose Hexacore

Our approach is grounded in engineering depth. We don't just pass or fail a machine; we model its errors. By identifying the root cause of volumetric deviation, we provide you with the data required to compensate your controller, adjust mechanical interfaces, and sustain peak accuracy over time.

Linear Laser Calibration

🔬 Precision Calibration & Testing Operations
01 📋 Service Details & Technical Specifications

High-precision linear laser interferometry measures positioning error, backlash, systematic positioning deviation, and repeatability along linear axes. Automatically generates pitch error compensation data for CNC controllers.

02 ⚙️ Types of Calibrated Machines & Equipment

Engineered for high accuracy across a broad spectrum of manufacturing CNC machines and inspection equipment:

Vertical Machining Centers (VMC)
Horizontal Machining Centers (HMC)
CNC Turning Centers (Lathes)
5-Axis Machining Centers
Gantry & Plano Milling Machines
Double Column CNCs
03 📜 Applicable Standards & International Compliance

Fully compliant with internationally recognized machine tool accuracy and metrology testing standards:

ISO 230-2 – Determination of positioning accuracy & repeatability of NC linear axes.
VDI/DGQ 3441 – Statistical evaluation of operational and positioning accuracy of machine tools.
JIS B 6201 – Test code for performance & geometric accuracy inspection of machine tools.
04 📊 Importance of Clear Technical Reports

Generates pitch error compensation tables directly formatted for Fanuc, Siemens & Heidenhain CNC controllers. Provides positioning accuracy curves and repeatability bands to eliminate dimensional errors on machined parts.
